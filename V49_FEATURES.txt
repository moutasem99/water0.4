V49 payment-flow update

- Payment method is no longer shown permanently on the sales screen.
- Tap "Confirm sale and save invoice".
- A dedicated confirmation modal shows the invoice total and asks:
  * Cash (نقدي)
  * Click (كليك)
- Selecting either method immediately saves the invoice with that payment method.
- +/−, in-app numpad, PDF/image/share, Excel, onboarding, launcher icons and all V48 features remain.
- Normal scrolling remains unchanged.
