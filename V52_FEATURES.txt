V52 — tutorial-only update

No sales UI, cart, top bar, prices, reports or business logic was redesigned.

Interactive tutorial now also teaches:

INVENTORY
- Navigate to Inventory.
- Explain current worker used for matching.
- Tap a real inventory count field.
- Use the in-app numpad to enter a sample quantity.
- Tap the morning/evening Save Inventory button.
- Tutorial intercepts the save so training data is NOT written.

MORNING SHIFT HANDOFF
- Return to Sales after the morning inventory walkthrough.
- Highlight the real Morning Shift Handoff button.
- Open the real handoff form.
- Explain:
  1. Actual morning cash
  2. Evening opening float
  3. Receiving evening worker
- Tap Confirm Handoff as a training action.
- Tutorial intercepts confirmation so the real shift is NOT changed.

EVENING
- If the app is already in evening shift, tutorial explains that morning was already handed off.
- It then teaches evening inventory and saving it before day close.

Safety
- Tutorial restores temporary cart and inventory-entry state on finish/skip.
- No demo invoice, demo inventory record or demo shift handoff is persisted.
