V54 — visual refinement update

SETTINGS
- New two-column settings layout on tablets/desktop; one column on phones.
- Cleaner header and grouped visual hierarchy.
- New theme picker with labeled palettes and 3-color previews.
- Added Sky, Indigo, Amber and Slate themes while preserving existing themes.
- Custom colors moved into an expandable advanced section.
- Products, inventory, customers, maintenance, backup and reset sections visually clarified.
- Existing controls and IDs are preserved.

REPORTS
- Cash sales are now the primary large number.
- Click and total sales are secondary.
- Expenses, wages, personal draws and operating net profit are compact KPIs.
- No accounting formula was changed.

INVOICE COLORS
- Cash invoices: soft green + green right rail.
- Click invoices: soft blue + blue right rail.
- Cancelled invoices: soft red/gray + red right rail.

DAY CLOSE SUMMARY
- Completely redesigned.
- Cash from sales is the main number.
- Click, total sales, expenses, draws, wages and coupons are compact.
- Separate morning/evening cash-reconciliation cards show:
  opening float, cash sales, expected cash, actual cash and cash difference.
- PDF, image and share remain directly accessible.
- Final close button is visually separated as a destructive/final action.

All V53 export fixes, product images, tutorial behavior, POS logic and normal scrolling remain.
