V55 — Night Mode

Added a complete appearance system:
- Light / Day mode
- Dark / Night mode
- Automatic mode following the Android/device dark-mode preference

Settings:
- New "App appearance" card with visual previews.
- Current resolved appearance is shown.
- Existing color themes remain independent of day/night mode.

Dark mode covers:
- Sales
- Product grid
- Cart
- In-app numpad
- Payment confirmation
- Inventory
- Reports
- Cash/Click invoice colors
- Archive
- Settings
- Tutorial
- Modals
- Day-close summary
- Bottom navigation

Cash invoices remain green-tinted and Click invoices blue-tinted in dark mode.
Print/export pages remain light for readability.

Existing V54 functions and business logic are unchanged.
